01 · The short answer

Pick by who does the entries.

The biggest difference between these tools isn't the price or the feature list. It's who books the entries: you, or the software.

  • You want the software to do the bookkeeping, and your CA to check it: GeniusCFO. It was built for AI to book the entries, keep the books current every day, prepare GST, TDS and income tax, and answer questions from your books. It also connects to Tally, so you don't have to stop using it.
  • Your accountant already lives in Tally and only wants faster data entry: TallyPrime with its new document reading, or an add-on such as Vyapar TaxOne that feeds entries into Tally.
  • You mostly send GST invoices and want a free start: Zoho Books (free under ₹25 lakh revenue), Vyapar, Refrens or Khatabook.
  • You only need GST filing at scale: Clear (ClearTax). It is compliance software, not a full set of books.

02 · The real difference

AI added on, or AI from the start?

Most accounting software in India was built 10 to 30 years ago for a person to type entries into. AI is now being added around that design. GeniusCFO started from the other end.

AI added on

The software you already know, with helpers

The ledger is still built around typing. The AI reads a document and fills in a voucher, or suggests a category, or answers a support question. You still run the books. Usually the AI is one feature, sometimes on a higher plan.

  • Books are as current as the last time someone sat down to update them.
  • Reports show what has been entered, when you go and look.
  • Tax work is a separate step, often in a separate tool.
AI from the start

GeniusCFO: the AI does the entries, you check them

GeniusCFO was designed for the AI to do the bookkeeping. Documents, bank statements and Tally vouchers come in, and the entries are booked with debits and credits, the document attached. A person reviews. The judgement stays human.

  • Books stay current every day, so warnings come early: a late customer, a GST mismatch, cash running low.
  • Ask GeniusCFO a question in plain English and get the number from your own books.
  • GST, TDS and income tax are prepared from the same books. Your CA files.

Why this matters: if the AI is a helper, your books are only as up to date as your busiest week allows. If the AI does the work, the books keep up on their own and your time goes into checking, not typing.

04 · Method

How we compared.

We read each tool's own website and pricing page, and public reporting about it, in November 2026.

  1. Where the AI sits

    Does the AI do the bookkeeping, or help a person do it? Is it a core part of the product or one feature on a higher plan?

  2. Indian tax

    GST returns, and whether TDS and income tax are covered in the same place.

  3. Your existing setup

    Can you bring Tally or other books across, or keep using them?

  4. Published price

    The starting price the vendor publishes, before GST. Prices change, so check the vendor's page.

  5. The review step

    Can your accountant or CA see and check the work?

What we did not do: run an accuracy test across tools, or give stars or scores. We don't think a vendor should grade its competitors. Try any tool on your own books before you decide.

07 · Before you pick

7 questions to ask before you pick.

  1. Who does the entries?

    If the answer is "you", the AI is a helper. That's fine if you have the time. If you don't, look for software where the AI books the entries and you check them.

  2. Can I try it on my own books?

    A demo company always looks good. Your own bills and bank statement show the truth. GeniusCFO's trial is ₹9 for 7 days on your own books.

  3. Do I have to leave Tally?

    Moving books is the step people dread. Ask whether the tool can read Tally, and whether it keeps reading it afterwards.

  4. How current are the numbers?

    Books updated once a month tell you about problems a month late. Ask how often the books catch up on their own.

  5. Are GST, TDS and income tax in one place?

    Three tools for three taxes means three sets of numbers to match. One set of books is simpler.

  6. Can my CA check the work?

    Good software makes your CA's review easier, not harder. Each entry should show the document it came from.

  7. Is the price clear?

    Check what is included, what is extra, and whether the AI is only on the top plan.

08 · Questions

Questions.

What is the best AI accounting software in India?

It depends on who you want doing the entries. If you want the software to do the bookkeeping and your CA to check it, we built GeniusCFO for that. If you want to keep typing entries into Tally with some help, TallyPrime 7.1's document reading or an add-on like Vyapar TaxOne fit better. We make GeniusCFO, so weigh our view accordingly.

What does AI-native accounting mean?

It means the software was designed for the AI to do the bookkeeping, with a person reviewing. Older software was designed for a person to type entries, and AI has been added around it as a helper.

Does AI accounting software replace my CA?

No. GeniusCFO prepares the books and the tax working. Your accountant or CA reviews it, keeps professional judgement and files. The work is automated. The judgement stays human.

Can I keep using Tally with GeniusCFO?

Yes. Tally Connect reads your Tally books every 15 minutes while Tally is open, and never changes anything in Tally. You can also upload Tally XML files once.

Does Tally have AI now?

Yes. TallyPrime 7.1, released in June 2026, added TallyIra, including a tool that reads business documents and turns them into entries. Tally has also said it plans to rebuild its core for AI.

Is there free AI accounting software in India?

Some tools have free plans, such as Zoho Books for businesses under ₹25 lakh revenue, and the basic versions of Vyapar, Refrens, hisabkitab and Khatabook. GeniusCFO's trial costs ₹9 for 7 days of full access.

Which AI accounting software handles GST, TDS and income tax together?

GeniusCFO prepares GST returns, TDS and income tax with advance tax from one set of books. Many other tools cover GST well; check each vendor's page for TDS and income tax.

How do I switch accounting software in the middle of the year?

Bring your opening balances and the year's entries across, then run both side by side for a few weeks. With GeniusCFO you can connect Tally or upload Tally or Zoho files, and nothing is booked until you approve it.
