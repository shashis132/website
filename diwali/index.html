01 · Why credits

Why is AI on your books metered at all?

Because the work arrives one document at a time: a bill photographed on a phone, a supplier invoice in an email, a bank line nobody has matched.

  1. 01 · ReadGeniusCFO reads each bill, invoice, statement and bank line, scans and photos included.
  2. 02 · PostIt posts each one to your books with debits and credits, with GST worked out in the posting.
  3. 03 · MatchIt matches bank activity to the books.
  4. 04 · AskAsk GeniusCFO answers questions from your own books, with the source document.

Each of those steps draws on the AI allowance in your plan, and the tiers differ in how much they carry.
