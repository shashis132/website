Why the dashboard and the bank never agree.

The dashboard tile shows sales less the fees it knows about. The bank shows what landed after commission, TCS, returns and reversals, on each platform's own cycle. Neither is your profit. The books, current every day, are where the two meet.

09 · Questions

Questions sellers ask first.

Does GeniusCFO connect to Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho?
No. There is no marketplace connector. You upload the settlement, payout and TCS exports you already download, with your bank statement and bills, and GeniusCFO books every line in them. Documents of any kind go in; current books come out.
Which files do I need each month?
The settlement report, payout statement and TCS report from each marketplace, the returns file if you have one, your purchase bills and your bank statement. CSV, Excel, PDF, scans and photos are all read.
Does my CA still file my GST?
Yes. GeniusCFO prepares the GST information from your books: output GST, input tax credit, net payable, return status and mismatches. Your CA reviews the same books in the same workspace and files the returns. Nothing here replaces your CA.
How are returns, reversals and TCS handled?
They are booked from the lines in your exports as entries with debits and credits, each carrying its source line, and matched against the payout that landed in the bank. Your profit figure is read off those books, not off a dashboard tile.
What does the ₹9 trial include?
Seven days of full access to everything, bookkeeping, GST information, bank matching, alerts and Ask GeniusCFO, on your own books. You pay ₹9 once on Razorpay, sign up, and start the same day. There is no UPI mandate and nothing is charged automatically afterwards.
What happens after the seven days?
Nothing is charged automatically. You choose a plan inside the product. If you don't, the AI stops while your books, statements and GST screens keep working, and nothing is deleted.
Why does the trial cost ₹9?
Because a free trial brought in fake sign-ups and people who worked through the AI credits meant for trials without any intention of using the product. A small payment keeps the trial for real businesses and keeps the AI allowance available to them.
Can I use GeniusCFO on my phone?
Yes. GeniusCFO works on phone and desktop, so you can check business numbers, alerts and answers wherever you are, and ask questions by voice.
