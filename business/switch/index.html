03 · GST, TDS and income tax

Three taxes. One set of books.

Because tax is handled when each entry is booked, the GST, TDS and income tax information is already prepared when it is time to file. Your accountant checks it and files. Nothing is filed without a person.

TDS, booked with the entry.

When a bill that attracts TDS is booked, the deduction is recorded with it. The TDS workspace shows what was deducted this month, what is due, the interest building up on anything late, and whether the books tie to the register. Then it tells you what to do now.

Income tax, from the books as they stand.

The year's income tax is estimated from the same current books, and the next advance tax instalment is worked out with its date and the interest that starts if it is missed. Your accountant starts from numbers that are already reconciled, not from a shoebox in July.

09 · Questions

Questions people ask before they switch.

Do I have to stop using my current software?
No. If you use Tally, connect it and keep working in it: GeniusCFO reads your vouchers every 15 minutes and never changes anything in Tally. If you use something else, keep it running and upload the same bank statement and documents here. Compare the two side by side for 7 days.
What do I need to bring?
One of three things. A Tally connection, set up in about five minutes on the computer that runs TallyPrime. Or Tally XML exports or a Zoho Books backup, uploaded once. Or your bank statement, invoices, bills and receipts as files: CSV, Excel, PDF, scans and photos are all read.
Does GeniusCFO change anything in Tally?
No. The program only reads from Tally, and it talks to Tally only on the same computer. Nothing is booked in GeniusCFO until a person approves the import, and each Tally voucher is booked exactly once, so uploading the same files again never doubles an entry.
I run a restaurant on Petpooja. Do I have to upload sales every day?
No. Connect the Gmail or Zoho inbox that receives your Petpooja sales reports, and every new report is read and booked to your company as it arrives. There is no upload and no manual entry.
Does my accountant have to change anything?
No. Your accountant reviews the same books in the same workspace you use, with the source document attached to every entry, and still files your returns. Nothing here replaces your accountant.
What does the ₹9 trial include?
Seven days of full access to everything, bookkeeping, GST, TDS and income tax information, bank matching, alerts and Ask GeniusCFO, on your own books. You pay ₹9 once on Razorpay, sign up, and start the same day. There is no UPI mandate and nothing is charged automatically afterwards.
What happens after the seven days?
Nothing is charged automatically. You choose a plan inside the product. If you don't, the AI stops while your books, statements and GST screens keep working, and nothing is deleted.
Why does the trial cost ₹9?
Because a free trial brought in fake sign-ups and people who worked through the AI credits meant for trials without any intention of using the product. A small payment keeps the trial for real businesses and keeps the AI allowance available to them.
Is my data safe if I decide not to buy a plan?
Yes. Nothing is deleted. The AI stops, and your books, statements and GST screens keep working, so you can still open and read everything you uploaded.
Can I use GeniusCFO on my phone?
Yes. GeniusCFO works on phone and desktop, so you can check business numbers, alerts and answers wherever you are, and ask questions by voice.
