08 · FAQ

FAQs

What does GeniusCFO do for a business?
GeniusCFO automates bookkeeping, prepares GST information, matches bank transactions, shows important business numbers and alerts, and answers financial questions from updated accounts.
Do I need accounting knowledge to use GeniusCFO?
No. Business owners can see clear dashboards and ask questions in everyday English. Your accountant or CA can review the accounting entries and source records.
Can I ask GeniusCFO questions by voice?
Yes. Ask GeniusCFO accepts typed or voice questions about profit, cash, GST and other financial information in the books.
Does GeniusCFO prepare GST information?
Yes. GeniusCFO uses updated books to prepare GST information, including output GST, input tax credit, net payable, return status and mismatches for review.
Can I use GeniusCFO on my phone and laptop?
Yes. GeniusCFO works on phone and desktop, so you can check business numbers, alerts and answers wherever you are.
Can my accountant or CA review the same books?
Yes. Your accountant or CA can review the entries, source records, reports and GST information in the same accounting workspace.
What does the free trial include?
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with full access to everything from day one. No credit card or UPI mandate is required to begin.
