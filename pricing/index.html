07 · FAQ

Questions about GeniusCFO pricing.

How long is the free trial?
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required, and you get full access to everything from day one.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
You choose a plan and pay via Razorpay. If you don’t, the AI stops — your books, statements, GST screens and manual entry all keep working, and nothing is ever deleted.
How does 3-month billing work?
The 3-month option is billed once for three months of access and saves 20% against the monthly rate. Monthly billing is available on every plan.
What is the difference between the plans?
Every plan includes the full product — books, GST, TDS, ITR, unlimited workflows and the Intelligence Hub. The tiers differ in how much AI you get, the level of support, and how many companies and members are included.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes. Contact the GeniusCFO team to move to a different plan. The new plan and billing amount will apply from the confirmed change date.
Is GST included in the displayed prices?
No. Plan prices exclude 18% GST. The total including GST is shown against the selected plan, and GeniusCFO provides a GST tax invoice for the billed amount.
Are managed accounting services included in a plan?
No. Accountant+, CA+ and the Independent Statutory & Tax Auditor service are optional services for business customers and are billed separately.
How are plans for CA firms priced?
Firm plans are priced by the client companies and team members included. Team covers three client companies and Enterprise covers ten. Additional client companies are ₹599 per month each and additional members are ₹299 per month each.
