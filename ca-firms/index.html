02 · What it prepares

The preparation, done before your team opens it.

GeniusCFO prepares bookkeeping entries, GST information, statements and reconciliations, and keeps the source record attached to every one.

Entries with debits and credits.

Not a summary — the accounting. Invoices, bills and statements come back as posted journal entries with the narration and the source document still attached.

  • Dr/Cr lines against real ledgers
  • Source document on every entry
  • Posted, editable, reversible

GST prepared from the books.

No separate working file. GST liability, input tax credit, mismatches and prepared GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B information, in the same client workspace.

  • Output GST and ITC reconciled
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B prepared
  • Mismatches flagged before filing

Bank matched to the books.

Books against the bank’s own closing balance. Every account, every period, with the gap explained rather than left for someone to find.

  • Per-account reconciliation summary
  • Differences explained, not just listed
  • Unpresented items carried forward

The client’s numbers, without waiting for the close.

Advisory needs current books. Profit, cash, receivables, payables and working capital update as the client’s books do — so the conversation can happen in August, not October.

  • Live profit and cash position
  • Receivables and payables ageing
  • Alerts raised as they appear

Ask instead of hunting through reports.

Type it or say it. Ask a question of the client’s books and get the figure with the entries it traces to — fewer clicks than opening the ledger.

  • Typed or voice questions
  • Figures traced to posted entries
  • Follow-ups in context

04 · Review and sign-off

Automated work. Your professional judgement.

Preparation stops being manual. Review, judgement and sign-off stay with the firm.

It ties out before you open it.

Total debits against total credits, the difference, and the count of anything still unposted — on the screen, not at the end of an afternoon.

The account structure is yours.

Heads, balances, tax mapping and the movement behind each account, editable by your team rather than fixed by the software.

Every figure traces to a document.

Notes sit with the statements, and each entry keeps the invoice, bill or statement it came from — so the review is a review, not a reconstruction.

09 · FAQ

Questions firms ask first.

What accounting work can GeniusCFO automate?
GeniusCFO automates routine bookkeeping entries, prepares GST information, financial statements and accounting reports, supports bank reconciliation, and keeps source references available for review.
Can my team review entries before sign-off?
Yes. Your team can review the prepared entries and their source records. Your firm keeps professional judgement and final sign-off.
Which financial statements can GeniusCFO prepare?
GeniusCFO prepares the Statement of Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement and Notes to Accounts. Trial Balance is available as a separate accounting report.
How does GeniusCFO help with GST?
It brings GST liability, input tax credit, mismatches and prepared GSTR-3B information into the client workspace for review.
Can I use voice input?
Yes. You can type a financial question or use voice input in Ask GeniusCFO.
How are CA/Firm plans priced?
Firm plans are priced by the client companies and team members included. Team covers three client companies and Enterprise covers ten. Additional client companies are 599 rupees per month each and additional members are 299 rupees per month each. Monthly and 3-month billing are available.
How does the firm trial work?
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with full access from day one. No credit card or UPI mandate is required to begin.
Do CA/Firm plans include Accountant+ or CA+?
No. Those managed accounting services are for business customers. CA/Firm plans are for firms that provide accounting and professional services to their own clients.
