01 · Which way should you use?

Connect once, or upload files yourself.

Both ways put the same entries in your books, and both let you check everything before it is booked. The difference is what happens after the first time.

Recommended

Connect Tally

You install a small GeniusCFO program on the Windows computer where TallyPrime runs. It reads your books from Tally, and then checks Tally every 15 minutes while Tally is open. New, edited and deleted vouchers come across without you exporting anything again.

  • Best when you keep working in Tally day to day.
  • Needs a Windows computer with TallyPrime on it.
  • Your books in GeniusCFO stay current with Tally.
One time

Upload XML files

You export your Masters, Day Book and Trial Balance from TallyPrime as XML files, and upload them in GeniusCFO. This brings your books across once, up to the day you exported them.

  • Best when you are moving from Tally to GeniusCFO.
  • Works from any computer that has the files.
  • Later changes in Tally need a fresh export.

Both start from Settings › Imports & Backup. On a new company, the Home page also shows this line; Import my books takes you there.

02 · Before you start

A few things to check first.

  1. Sign in as the owner or an executive

    Connecting Tally, and approving an import, are open to the company's owner or executive. A team member with the accountant role can upload files and review the account mapping, and the owner or an executive then approves.

  2. Make sure you are in the right company

    If you have more than one company in GeniusCFO, switch to the one these books belong to. GeniusCFO compares the Tally company's name with this company before it lets the books in, so another business's books cannot land here by mistake.

  3. Complete your company profile

    GeniusCFO asks for your legal address, pincode and the other required details before most screens open. Finish them in Settings › Company.

  4. Find the right screen

    Everything in this guide starts from Settings › Imports & Backup. You will see three tiles: Connect Tally, Upload files and Export & backup.

03 · Connect Tally

Connect Tally, step by step.

Do these steps on the Windows computer where TallyPrime is installed. It takes about five minutes, plus the time Tally needs to send your books.

Get TallyPrime ready

  1. Open TallyPrime and load your company

    The program can only read a company that is open in Tally.

  2. Let TallyPrime answer on this computer

    In TallyPrime press F1 (Help) › Settings › Connectivity, and set TallyPrime to act as a server on port 9000 (this is TallyPrime's usual setting). This lets the GeniusCFO program ask Tally for your books on this same computer. Nothing outside the computer can reach Tally through it.

Install the GeniusCFO program

  1. Download it

    In GeniusCFO, open Settings › Imports & Backup › Connect Tally and press Download for Windows. The file is called GeniusCFO-Tally.exe.

  2. Run it

    Double-click the file. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for programs it has not seen many times before.

    The program installs itself for your Windows user only. It does not need administrator rights, and it starts by itself each time you sign in to Windows.

  3. Look for the GeniusCFO icon near the clock

    The program runs quietly in the system tray, at the bottom-right of the screen. If you cannot see it, click the small ^ arrow there. Hovering over it shows its status, for example “GeniusCFO Tally: Connected to Tally”.

Link it to your GeniusCFO company

  1. Enter the code the program shows

    The program shows a short code, such as ABCD-2345. Under Enter the code it shows, enter it and press Connect.

    If the GeniusCFO page is open in a browser on the same computer, the code fills in by itself. A code works for a short while; if it runs out, the program shows a new one.

  2. Choose which Tally company this is

    The card now says “Connected. Choose which Tally company this is” and lists the companies open in Tally, each with the date its books start. Pick the one that holds this company's books. The button then shows its name, for example Use Sws. Press it.

    If your company is not in the list, open it in TallyPrime (F3, Select Company). The list updates by itself.

  3. Bring your books in

    Under Bring your books in, press Read my books from Tally. You can enter a From date; leave it blank to start from the date your books begin in Tally.

    The program reads your accounts (Tally's masters), then your Day Book one month at a time, then your Trial Balance. The card shows the progress, for example “Reading your books from Tally: 5 of 14 files. Keep Tally open.”

  4. Review and approve the import

    When the files are read, an import opens below the card: “Your books are read. Review them in the import below. Nothing is booked until you approve.” This is the same review as an upload. Section 07 explains each step: the account mapping, the dry run, approving, posting and finishing.

Nothing is booked until you approve. The program only reads from Tally. It never changes anything in Tally, and the books in GeniusCFO change only after a person approves the import.

04 · After you connect

How your books stay in step with Tally.

Once the first import is finished, GeniusCFO looks at Tally every 15 minutes while Tally and the program are open. Each look compares Tally's vouchers with your books and brings across only what changed.

1 · MastersNew and renamed ledgers 2 · Voucher listWhat is new, edited or gone 3 · Day BookOnly the vouchers that changed 4 · Once a dayTrial Balance compared with Tally

What happens to each kind of change

A voucher you already posted is editedUnder Save as draft, your books keep the posted entry and the card asks you to Apply change. When you apply it, the old entry is reversed and the new one posted. Under Post automatically, this happens at once.

Save as draft, or post automatically

Under When something changes in Tally you choose how new entries arrive. New connections start on Save as draft.

  • Save as draft: new entries wait in Journal Entries › Drafts. Check each one and press Post, or tick several and press Post selected. A change to an entry you already posted waits on the card until you apply it.
  • Post automatically: new and changed entries go straight into your books as soon as they are read.

Opening balances

Opening balances are posted once, when your books first come in. They are dated the day your books start (usually 1 April). If you read your books while the Tally company was still empty, the opening balances arrive on the first look after you enter them in Tally, as one draft entry for you to check.

Safety stop. If Tally suddenly shows far fewer vouchers than before, or a different company with the same name, GeniusCFO stops and changes nothing. The card says “Your books were not changed” and why. Check that the right company is open in Tally. If the change is what you meant, press Carry on.

06 · Upload XML files

Upload Tally XML files, step by step.

Use this if you are not connecting Tally, or want to bring books across once. You need three files from TallyPrime; the third is optional but recommended.

Export the files from TallyPrime

  1. Masters (your ledgers and groups)

    In TallyPrime: Export › Masters › All Masters, and choose XML as the format.

  2. Day Book (your vouchers)

    Open Display › Day Book, set the period to the whole period you want (press F2 to change it), then Export as XML.

  3. Trial Balance (optional, recommended)

    Open the Trial Balance in the detailed, ledger-wise view and Export as XML. GeniusCFO uses it to check that the books it built match Tally's.

Export the files from TallyPrime itself. A file made for loading into Tally has no Tally IDs on its vouchers, and GeniusCFO cannot use it. Export one financial year at a time. Each XML file can be up to 500 MB, and a Zoho Books backup .zip up to 200 MB.

Upload them

  1. Open Upload files

    Settings › Imports & Backup › Upload files.

  2. Drop the files, or press Choose files

    You can add all three at once. Under This upload each file gets one line: “Added to this import”, or the reason it was not added.

  3. Add anything you missed

    While the import is open, press Add more files at the bottom of it, for example to add the Trial Balance after the other two.

  4. Review, approve, post and finish

    The import opens on the same page. The next section explains each step.

07 · Reviewing an import

What each step of the review means.

Whether the files came from the Tally program or from your upload, the import shows five steps at the top: Files read · Accounts mapped · Dry run passed · Approved · Posted & finalized. Nothing reaches your books before Approved.

  1. Files read

    Each file is listed with what was found in it, for example “70 accounts, 4 with opening balances” or “53 vouchers”. A file that could not be read says why. Use Remove to take a wrong file out.

  2. Books start on

    Enter the first day your books in GeniusCFO cover, usually 1 April. Opening balances are dated that day.

  3. Accounts mapped

    Every Tally ledger is matched to an account in GeniusCFO's chart of accounts, using its Tally group. Customers and suppliers get their own accounts. The table shows each ledger, its Tally group, its opening balance and the account chosen. Use the filters Needs a decision, Customers, Suppliers and With an opening balance, and press change to choose a different account. The opening balances row at the bottom shows whether they balance.

  4. Run the dry run

    Press Run the dry run. GeniusCFO works out every entry without booking anything, and says either “Dry run: ✓ ready to approve” or what still needs your attention, such as a ledger without an account or vouchers dated before your books start.

  5. Approve import

    The owner or an executive presses Approve import, then confirms with Yes, approve. If Tally sent no entries at all, the question says so, so that books are not started empty by mistake.

  6. Post to the books

    Press Post to the books. While it works, the panel says “Still posting”. When it finishes it says “Posted. The books match the files you imported.” and shows the dates your entries cover, with Open in Journal to see them.

  7. Verify & finalize

    Check a few entries, then press Verify & finalize. Until you do, Roll back everything removes every entry this import posted, if something is wrong.

What comes across from Tally

  • Every voucher as a journal entry with its debits and credits: sales, purchases, receipts, payments, contra, journals, credit notes and debit notes.
  • Sales and purchase vouchers become invoices and bills for the party named. Receipts, payments, notes and journals made against a bill in Tally settle that bill here too, so its paid, part-paid or unpaid status matches Tally.
  • Opening balances, once, dated the day your books start.
  • Every imported entry carries an Imported mark, so you always know where it came from.

Before you approve, you can press Discard to drop the import completely. Your books do not change.

09 · Questions

Questions.

Does GeniusCFO change anything in Tally?

No. The program only reads from Tally, using a fixed set of export requests. It has no way to write to Tally.

Is my data safe on the way to GeniusCFO?

The program only makes outgoing connections to GeniusCFO, over encrypted HTTPS. It opens nothing on your network for others to reach. It talks to Tally only on the same computer.

Can an entry be booked twice?

No. Each Tally voucher carries its own ID, and GeniusCFO keeps exactly one entry for each one. Uploading the same files again, or uploading files for a period the program already brought across, finds the vouchers already in your books and does not book them again.

Can I use both ways?

Yes, though not at the same moment. The sync waits while an upload is being reviewed, and carries on once it is finished or discarded.

What if I filed a GST return for a month, and a voucher in it changes in Tally?

GeniusCFO does not change a filed period on its own. It holds the change and lists it for you, with the reason, so you can correct it deliberately.

Is stock brought across?

Your vouchers and ledgers are. Stock values that Tally works out from its inventory are not, and they are left out of the daily Trial Balance check so they do not show as a difference.

Does the Tally program work on a Mac?

No. TallyPrime runs on Windows, so the program is for Windows. If your Tally runs elsewhere, upload the XML files instead.

Does my CA still review the books?

Yes. GeniusCFO prepares the entries; nothing is booked until a person approves the import, and drafts wait for review. Your CA keeps final sign-off.
